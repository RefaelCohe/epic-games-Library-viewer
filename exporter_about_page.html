Epic Games Library Exporter

Export your complete Epic Games purchase history, with every game, date and price, or open it straight in the Library Viewer. Epic doesn't offer this anywhere on its website.

Add to Chrome Open the Library Viewer

How it works

Three steps, all inside your browser.

  1. Sign in to EpicOpen accounts.epicgames.com and sign in as usual.
  2. Click ExportOpen the extension, choose the fields and date range you want, and click Export Library.
  3. Download or viewWatch the live progress, then download a file or open your library in the Library Viewer.

Features

Everything you need to keep your own record of your library.

JSON, CSV or text

Structured JSON with totals per currency and year, a CSV that opens in Excel or Google Sheets, or plain text.

Choose your fields

Game name, date, price, discount, total, tax, order ID, quantity, payment method, gift recipient and order type.

Filter by date

Export everything, or only the purchases from a period you choose.

Live progress

A progress bar that follows Epic's real pace, with the time remaining. It retries on its own if Epic is busy.

Open in the Library Viewer

See your games with covers, stats and full order details in one click, with no file to save or upload.

Chrome and Chromium browsers

Works in Google Chrome, and in Chromium-based browsers that can install extensions from the Chrome Web Store, such as Edge, Brave and Opera.

Your data stays in your browser

  • Reads your order history only when you click Export.
  • No servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Opening the Library Viewer passes the data inside Chrome, once. It's deleted right after.
