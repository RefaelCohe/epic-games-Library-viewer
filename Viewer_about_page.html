Epic Games Library Viewer

See every game you own on Epic, with cover art, spending stats and full order details. It runs entirely in your browser.

Open the Library Viewer Get the Exporter extension

Features

A clear view of a library Epic only shows inside its launcher.

Cover art

Games appear with their covers, from Steam or Wikipedia. They load page by page and are remembered for next time.

Stats that add up

Games claimed for free, money spent and saved, kept separate per currency, plus games per year.

Search, filter and sort

Find a game instantly. Filter by purchased, free or gifted, by date or by amount paid.

Full order details

Click a game to see its order: every item, list price, discount, tax, total and order ID.

Export anytime

Download your library as JSON, CSV or TXT, in the same formats as the Exporter extension.

Dark and light

Dark by default, with a light theme or your system setting a click away. Works on phones too.

Questions

How do I get my library into the Viewer?

Install the Exporter extension, open your Epic account page and click Export. When it's done, choose "Open in Library Viewer". You can also load a JSON, CSV or TXT file you saved earlier by dropping it on the Viewer.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your file is read in your browser and is never uploaded. To find cover art, the Viewer looks up game names on Wikidata and Wikipedia, and loads the images from Steam's and Wikipedia's image servers. Nothing else about your purchases is sent.

Why do some games have no cover?

Covers come from Steam, and from Wikipedia for games that aren't on Steam, such as many Epic exclusives. If neither has the game, its initials are shown. A cover is only shown when the name matches exactly, so you never get the wrong one.

Does the Viewer remember my library?

Only while the tab is open, so you can reload or read these pages without losing it. Closing the tab clears it, or choose "Close library" from the menu to clear it right away.

Can I download my data from the Viewer?

Yes. Click Export to download JSON, CSV or TXT, with the same fields, date filter and file structure as the extension.

Private by design

  • Your file never leaves your browser.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • Only game names are looked up, to find cover art.
