Privacy Policy

For the Epic Games Library Exporter Chrome extension and the Epic Games Library Viewer website.

The short version

We don't collect your data. There are no servers, accounts, analytics or tracking. Your purchase history is read and processed in your own browser, and it goes only where you choose: a file on your computer, or the Library Viewer in another tab.

Who we are

The Epic Games Library Exporter ("the extension") and the Epic Games Library Viewer ("the Viewer") are independent projects by Refael Cohen. They are not affiliated with Epic Games, Inc. In this policy, "we" means the developer.

The Exporter extension

What it reads

The extension does nothing until you click Export Library while you're on your Epic Games account page. It then reads your order history from Epic, in that tab and with the sign-in you already have there:

  • game and item names, purchase dates and order IDs,
  • prices, discounts, taxes and totals,
  • the type of payment method used (for example "PayPal"), and gift recipients and order types when Epic provides them.

It never reads or stores your password, and it doesn't read other websites or your browsing activity.

Where the data goes

When the export finishes, you choose what happens next:

  • Download a file: the data is saved as a JSON, CSV or TXT file on your computer.
  • Open in the Library Viewer: the extension passes the data to the Viewer tab inside Chrome, not over the internet. Until the Viewer page asks for it, the data waits in Chrome's in-memory session storage for the extension. It is handed over once, then deleted immediately. Anything not picked up is deleted after 15 minutes, or when you close the browser.

The data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What it stores

  • Your export preferences (selected fields and default format), saved with chrome.storage.sync. If Chrome Sync is turned on, Google syncs them between your Chrome browsers.
  • A progress estimate: after a full export, the number of orders and the dates of your newest and oldest orders, saved locally with chrome.storage.local. It is used only to make the next progress bar accurate.

Both are removed when you uninstall the extension.

The Library Viewer website

  • Your files stay on your device. A file you load is read by your browser and is never uploaded.
  • While the tab is open, your library is kept in that tab's session storage, so it survives a reload. Closing the tab clears it, and so does "Close library" in the menu.
  • On your device, the Viewer remembers your theme, your export preferences and which cover image belongs to which game name, in your browser's local storage. You can clear this data in your browser settings at any time.
  • The Viewer uses no cookies, analytics, ads or tracking.

Third-party services

To work, the Viewer's pages make a few requests from your browser to these services. Like any website, they can see your IP address and the request itself:

  • Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation): game names are looked up to find cover art. Only the game name is sent, never prices, dates or order details. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Steam's image servers (Valve): the cover images are loaded from Steam's public image servers.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub): hosts the Viewer and these pages, and loads the developer photo on the About pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The extension itself only talks to Epic Games, to read your order history.

Sharing and selling

We don't collect your data, so we never share it, sell it or use it for advertising.

Transparency

The Library Viewer's source code is public on GitHub. The extension's code isn't minified, so anyone can inspect it in the extension's installed files.

Children

Neither product is directed at children under 13, and neither collects personal information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete exported files from your computer at any time.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove its preferences and progress estimate.
  • Clear the Viewer's data with "Close library", by closing the tab, or by clearing site data for refaelcohe.github.io in your browser.
